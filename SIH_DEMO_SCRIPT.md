# SIH 2026 demo script — production build

## 1. Welfare Officer

1. Sign in.
2. Run **Live ML Assessment**.
3. Show risk score, burnout score, confidence and trajectory.
4. Open **Why Now** and explain SHAP contributors.
5. Show the evidence gate and the escalation guard.
6. Open **Welfare Cases**.
7. Open a persistent case such as `WF-00041`.
8. Review the case and approve / modify / hold.
9. Open **Intervention**.
10. Run a workload + recovery **What-If** scenario.
11. Record an intervention.
12. Open **Alerts** and use **Review** to acknowledge the alert and open its case.
13. Open **Audit** and show the recorded review/intervention/alert events.

## 2. Commander

1. Sign out.
2. Sign in as Commander.
3. Show the aggregate welfare index, workload pressure and resource gap.
4. Open Workload and Reports.
5. Explain that individual welfare case detail is intentionally not visible.

## 3. Personnel

1. Sign out.
2. Sign in as Personnel.
3. Open Daily Pulse.
4. Save a voluntary pulse.
5. Open Privacy & Consent.
6. Toggle a consent control and save it.
7. Explain that optional inputs are purpose-limited and not a disciplinary score.

## Closing line

> AI detects. Evidence validates. Humans decide. Welfare intervenes. Sentinel measures recovery.
