# SENTINEL PULSE — 5–7 Minute SIH Video Script

## 00:00 — Problem and purpose
Show landing page.
Say: “SENTINEL PULSE is an AI-assisted predictive personnel stress and welfare monitoring system for uniformed forces, aligned to SIH PS-26186. It moves welfare from reactive checks to a Monitor → Detect → Predict → Explain → Support → Recover workflow.”

## 00:30 — Welfare Officer login
Login with welfare.officer@sentinel.local / Sentinel@123.

## 00:45 — Overview
Show active cases, intervention cases, alerts and the AI assessment view.
Mention: “The model is advisory; welfare decisions remain with authorized personnel.”

## 01:20 — Prediction Models
Click “Run Live ML Assessment”. Show risk score, tier, confidence and trajectory.
Then open “Why Now” and show contributing factors, evidence quality, persistence, corroboration and safe-to-escalate gate.

## 02:10 — Welfare Intervention Recommendation
Go to Intervention.
Run the workload/recovery scenario.
Show before → after risk score and the recommended human-led welfare route.
Record the intervention.

## 02:50 — Automated Alerts
Go to Alerts.
Open/review an alert.
Show that the alert is linked to a welfare case and that review is persisted.

## 03:30 — Case Management + Human Review
Open a case.
Approve / Modify / Hold it.
Show the status change.
Then mark a follow-up complete after intervention.

## 04:10 — Privacy + RBAC
Logout and login as Commander.
Show Force Welfare / Workload / Reports.
Point out that command sees aggregate welfare information rather than private individual detail.

Logout and login as Personnel.
Show Daily Pulse and Privacy & Consent.
Change a consent setting and save it.

## 05:10 — Security and audit
Return to Welfare Officer.
Open HRMS Adapter and Audit.
Show the audit timeline with case review/intervention events.

## 05:40 — PS mapping close
Say: “This prototype demonstrates the five core PS capabilities: predictive models, welfare intervention recommendations, role-based access and privacy, automated authorized alerts, and anonymization/secure storage. The bundled data is synthetic; approved anonymized force data and authorized HRMS integration are required before operational deployment.”
